argument-hint: "[ticker or company name]"
---

Load the `catalyst-research` skill and research the company given in the
arguments. If no company is given, ask which one.

Apply the sourcing discipline strictly: search for every figure, cite it, date
it, and label anything unverified. Apply the small-account filters (liquidity,
spread, binary risk) before spending effort on the thesis.

Finish with the thesis note format, then offer to size the trade with
`position-sizing`.
