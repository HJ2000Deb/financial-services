# Debrett's origination screen — criteria and weights

Adjust per sector, but start here. The point of a written screen is that two people
running the same sweep six weeks apart produce comparable lists.

## Hard filters — a name that fails these is out

| Filter | Default |
|---|---|
| EBITDA | £1m to £15m, or revenue £10m to £150m where EBITDA is not disclosed |
| Headquarters | UK. European headquarters only where the mandate says so |
| Ownership | Founder, family, management or sponsor-backed. Listed subsidiaries only on a carve-out thesis |
| Trading history | Three years of filed accounts, or two plus audited management accounts |
| Status | Not in administration, not in a live process we know of, no Debrett's conflict |

## Timing signals — scored 0 to 3, weighted

| Signal | Weight | 3 looks like | 0 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| Succession | 3 | Founder over 60, no successor in the management team, second-generation not in the business | Founder in their 40s, deep bench |
| Sponsor clock | 3 | Sponsor-backed since year 5+, fund past its investment period | Investment made in the last 18 months |
| Plateau | 2 | Two consecutive years of flat or falling revenue after a growth run | Compounding double-digit growth |
| Capital need | 2 | Capex or working capital requirement visible in the accounts, gearing already stretched | Net cash, low capital intensity |
| Consolidation | 2 | Three or more completed deals in the sub-sector in 18 months, named buyers | No observable buyer activity |
| Relationship | 2 | Existing Debrett's relationship, or a client or adviser who can introduce | Cold, no route in |

Weighted score out of 42. In practice:

- **28+** — Priority A. Call this month.
- **18–27** — Priority B. This quarter.
- **Below 18** — Priority C. Monitor.

Override rule: a 3 on succession or on the sponsor clock justifies a call regardless of
total score. A high score built only on consolidation and relationship does not.

## Fields to capture for every candidate

Company name and legal entity · sub-sector · registered number · revenue and EBITDA with
basis and source · employees · ownership and shareholders · sponsor and investment date ·
last filed accounts date · directors and ages where filed · signals scored with evidence ·
route in · source and date accessed for each figure.

## What must never be inferred

- That a company is for sale, or that the owner is considering a sale.
- A valuation or a multiple for the candidate.
- Shareholder identity or percentages not on the register.
- Anything about a named individual's intentions or personal circumstances beyond what is
  filed publicly.
