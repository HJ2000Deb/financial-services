# Weekly process update

The document that keeps a vendor calm. One page, same shape every week, so the vendor reads
the changes rather than the format.

## Reconcile before drafting

Never draft the update from the deal team's impression of the week. Pull the state:

1. **Approach log** — approaches made, responses received, since last update.
2. **NDA log** — signed this week, outstanding, IMs issued.
3. **Q&A log** — questions received, answered, outstanding, overdue.
4. **Timetable** — next three milestones and whether any has moved.
5. **Outlook and Teams** — anything material said to a buyer that is not yet in a log.

Where a log and the correspondence disagree, the update waits until someone resolves it.
An update built on a stale log is worse than a late update.

## Structure

**1. Where we stand** — two sentences. Stage, and whether we are on the timetable.

**2. This week** — three to six bullets, each a fact with a name and a date. Approaches
made, NDAs signed, IMs out, meetings held, questions answered.

**3. Buyer engagement** — the table, same columns every week:

| Buyer | Stage | Last contact | Next step | Owner | Date |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**4. What we need from you** — the specific asks, with dates. Never more than three. If
there are more than three, the deal team has a prioritisation problem to solve first.

**5. Next two weeks** — the milestones, with dates, and what would move them.

**6. Our read** — one short paragraph of judgement: what the engagement pattern tells us,
and any recommendation. This is the paragraph the vendor reads twice; a partner writes or
approves it.

## Rules

- One page. British English. Sterling. House register.
- Facts only in sections 1 to 5; judgement confined to section 6.
- No buyer's confidential information, and no view on price, in a document that could be
  forwarded.
- Never report an approach as made, or a document as sent, unless a log or the mailbox
  confirms it.
- Drafted for partner review. Nothing is sent by Claude.

## Output

1. The update, one page, ready for partner review.
2. **Internal annex**, not for the vendor: reconciliation exceptions found, overdue Q&A
   items, and anything in the logs that contradicts the correspondence.
