# Pipeline review

The point is prioritisation, not reporting. The output is a ranked list of where the next
week goes and the decisions the partners must take to unblock it.

## Assemble the state

From the CRM, the mandate Projects and the deal folders — not from recollection:

- **Live mandates** — stage, next milestone, date, owner, and whether it moved this week.
- **Origination** — conversations open, last touch, next touch due, signal score.
- **Pitches** — outstanding pitches and decisions expected.
- **Fee events** — what is expected to invoice this quarter, and what it depends on.

Where the CRM and the correspondence disagree, the CRM is wrong. Flag it; do not
silently prefer one.

## The four tables

**1. Live mandates**

| Mandate | Stage | Next milestone | Date | Moved this week | Blocked on | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

**2. Stuck** — anything with no movement in 14 days, or a milestone slipped twice. For each:
one line on why, and the single action that would unstick it.

**3. Decisions for the partners** — the specific decisions, with the options and our
recommendation. Not "discuss".

**4. Origination priorities** — the ten conversations worth time this week, ranked, each
with the reason and the next touch.

## The recommendation

Close with three lines: where the team's marginal hour is best spent this week, what we
should stop doing, and the one thing that will look worse in a fortnight if untouched.

## Guardrails

- Facts from systems, judgement labelled as judgement.
- No fee, valuation or probability figure invented to fill a column. Blank with an owner
  beats a guess.
- Internal only. Never leaves the firm, and never mixes one client's information into
  another's record.
