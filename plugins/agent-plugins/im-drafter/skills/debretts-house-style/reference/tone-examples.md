# Tone — before and after

House register, shown rather than described. Use these as the pattern for any drafting.

## Opening a market context note

**Not this**
> In today's rapidly evolving industrials landscape, M&A activity has been characterised
> by a dynamic interplay of strategic and financial buyers seeking to unlock value.

**This**
> Mid-market industrials changed hands on high single-digit EBITDA multiples through the
> first half, with sponsors taking a larger share of completed deals than in 2024. Two
> things follow for your business.

## Explaining a risk to a founder

**Not this**
> There may be some potential customer concentration considerations that could
> conceivably impact buyer perception in certain scenarios.

**This**
> Your largest customer is 34% of revenue. Every buyer will price that, and the better
> ones will ask for a contractual answer rather than a reassurance. We should have the
> answer ready before the first management meeting, not after it.

## Recommending against a buyer

**Not this**
> While this party could be interesting, there are some question marks.

**This**
> We would not approach them in the first wave. They completed a similar acquisition in
> March, the integration is not done, and an approach now invites a low anchor. Revisit
> in the second wave if we need to broaden.

## Flagging a gap in the evidence

**Not this**
> Multiples in the sector are around 8-9x.

**This**
> We have two directly comparable transactions, both 2024, both UK, at 7.8x and 8.4x
> reported EBITDA. A third at 11.0x is a different scale and a different growth profile,
> and we would not put it in front of a buyer as a comparable. `[UNVERIFIED]` — sponsor
> deal terms on the fourth are press-reported only.

## Writing an action from a meeting

**Not this**
> Team to consider follow-up on diligence matters as appropriate.

**This**
> AH to send the revised EBITDA bridge to Meridian by Thursday 3 September, with the
> R&D capitalisation note attached. Blocks their IC on 8 September.
