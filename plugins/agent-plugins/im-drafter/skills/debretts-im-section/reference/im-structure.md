# Debrett's IM structure

Default structure for a mid-market UK sell-side IM, 35 to 45 pages. Adjust the page
budget per mandate, but agree it before drafting.

| # | Section | Pages | What belongs in it | What does not |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Important notice | 1 | Confidentiality, no reliance, projections are the vendor's | Anything persuasive |
| 2 | Executive summary | 2 | The equity story pillars, investment highlights, transaction summary | New facts introduced nowhere else |
| 3 | Investment highlights | 1–2 | Five to seven evidenced claims | Adjectives doing a number's work |
| 4 | Company overview | 3–4 | History, legal structure, sites, headcount, what it actually does | Market commentary |
| 5 | Products and services | 4–5 | Revenue by line with basis, pricing model, delivery, IP and accreditations | Roadmap presented as fact |
| 6 | Customers | 3–4 | Concentration, tenure, contract structure, churn, win rates, named where permitted | Named customers without consent |
| 7 | Market | 3–4 | Size and growth with sources, structure, drivers, competitive set | Unsourced market size |
| 8 | Operations | 3–4 | Sites, capacity, supply chain, systems, quality, capex history | Aspirational operating model |
| 9 | Management and people | 2–3 | Structure, biographies, tenure, retention arrangements, continuity plan | Speculation on who stays |
| 10 | Financial review | 6–8 | Three years plus LTM, revenue and margin analysis with drivers, adjusted EBITDA bridge, working capital, capex, net debt | Unlabelled adjusted figures |
| 11 | Growth opportunities | 3–4 | Named, costed, sequenced, with the constraint on each | Growth that needs the vendor to stay |
| 12 | Projections | 2–3 | The vendor's projections, assumptions stated, attributed to the vendor | Debrett's own forecast |
| 13 | Transaction and process | 1–2 | What is for sale, structure, timetable, contact protocol | Price expectation |
| 14 | Appendices | as needed | Statutory accounts, organisation chart, site schedule, contract summaries | Anything load-bearing |

## Sequencing

Draft in this order, not in page order: 10 (financial review) → 4 → 5 → 6 → 7 → 8 → 9 →
11 → 12 → 3 → 2 → 13 → 1. The financial review sets the numbers every other section must
agree with; the executive summary can only be written once the rest exists.

## Page-budget discipline

If a section runs over budget, it has more than one job. Split it or cut it. Reducing the
type size or the margins breaks the brand standard and signals a document nobody edited.

## Labels that must appear on every financial figure

Period · statutory or management · audited or unaudited · reported or adjusted · currency
and units · source (workbook cell or document page).
