# Buyer scoring model

Five criteria, scored 1 to 5, weighted. Weights are the default for a UK mid-market
sell-side process; change them per mandate but record the change on the Summary tab so a
partner can see what drove the ranking.

| Criterion | Weight | 5 | 3 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Strategic fit** | 30% | The business fills a gap the buyer has named publicly | Plausible adjacency | Sector overlap only |
| **Ability to pay** | 25% | Balance sheet or dry powder comfortably covers the range, with precedent at this size | Capacity likely, no precedent at this size | Would need financing we cannot evidence |
| **Acquisitiveness** | 20% | Two or more completed deals in 24 months, corp dev team, stated programme | One deal, or a programme announced but unproven | No observable M&A |
| **Deliverability** | 15% | Decision makers reachable, clean process, no regulatory or ownership complication | Reachable, slower governance | Committee-bound, listed-parent approval, or hostile timetable |
| **Relationship** | 10% | Existing Debrett's relationship with the decision maker | Warm route via a client or adviser | Cold |

Weighted score out of 5. Indicative tiering: **4.0+** Tier 1 · **3.0–3.9** Tier 2 ·
**below 3.0** Tier 3.

## Overrides

Applied after scoring, and never applied silently — each is a flag for the vendor to
decide on:

- **Antitrust** — combined share in a defined market likely to attract a referral. Flag
  with the reason and the market definition used.
- **Confidentiality** — a customer, supplier or serial leaker whose knowledge of the sale
  would damage trading before it helps price.
- **Vendor exclusion** — a name the vendor has excluded. Keep it in the workbook, marked
  excluded, so a later wave does not rediscover it.
- **Fund life** — a sponsor past its investment period, or one that has just deployed into
  the sub-sector. Both change behaviour; note which.

## Fields per buyer

Name · legal entity · type (strategic / sponsor / sponsor-backed platform) · category ·
revenue or fund size with source · relevant acquisitions with dates and disclosed values ·
five criterion scores · weighted score · tier · flags · buyer-specific angle · contact,
title and route · relationship owner at Debrett's · source and date accessed for every
sourced field.

## What must not be scored from memory

Fund size, dry powder, cheque range, portfolio company ownership, deal values, corporate
development contacts. All of it comes from a connected source or is marked `[UNVERIFIED]`
and left out of the wave until confirmed.
